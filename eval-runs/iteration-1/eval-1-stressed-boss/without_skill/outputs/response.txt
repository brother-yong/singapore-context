That sounds really overwhelming, especially getting all of that dumped on you right before the weekend. It's completely understandable to feel stressed in that situation. Here are a few things that might help:

First, take a breath. You don't need to tackle everything tonight. Give yourself permission to step away and decompress — your brain will actually work better after some rest, and you'll have a clearer perspective on Monday morning.

When you do get back to it, try this approach:
- Brain dump: Write down all three projects and any details you remember. This gets it out of your head and onto paper.
- Prioritize: Which ones are actually urgent? Which have the earliest deadlines? This helps you figure out what actually needs attention first.
- Break it down: For each project, identify just the next immediate step. You don't need to solve everything at once — just the first small action.
- Talk to your boss: If it's genuinely unrealistic to do all three well, have a conversation early next week about timelines and priorities. Most managers don't realize how their requests land until you point it out professionally.

For tonight though: close the laptop, do something that helps you decompress, and remember that Friday evening is supposed to be yours. The projects will still be there on Monday, and you'll be in a much better position to handle them after some rest.

You've got this. One thing at a time.
