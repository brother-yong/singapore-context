I'm sorry to hear that happened. That's a frustrating feeling, especially when you find out through social media instead of hearing about it from them directly. It can sting when friends make plans without including you, even if it wasn't intentional.

A few thoughts that might help:

1. They may not have realized you'd be available or interested. It's worth giving them the benefit of the doubt before assuming anything.

2. Consider reaching out to one of them in a calm way. You could say something like, "Hey, I saw you all went for hotpot! Would've been fun to join. Let me know next time?" This opens the door for conversation without sounding accusatory.

3. If this is part of a pattern where you're regularly left out, it might be worth having a deeper conversation with your close friends about feeling excluded. True friends will want to know if you're feeling hurt.

4. Use this as a gentle reminder to also reach out and initiate plans with your friends. Sometimes we all get caught up in our own schedules and forget to think of others.

Don't let this one instance ruin your friendships. Most friend groups have these moments. How you respond to it can actually strengthen your bonds if you handle it with honesty and care.
