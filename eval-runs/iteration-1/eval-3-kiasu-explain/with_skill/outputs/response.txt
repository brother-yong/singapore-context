Eh wah, kiasu is very Singapore one lah! It basically mean you always worry you lose out, so you must come first, get the best, always action action one. Like, queue 2 hours for something before anyone else, or when opportunity come one, you just rush rush go grab it, never mind what else happening.

Very common here lor. Singaporeans kiasu because, you know, small country right, everyone competing for everything — jobs, schools, reservations, parking. So people die die must be first mah, if not feel like they lose out already. You see lah, during sales or any free food thing, confirm the aunties and uncles there 5am already, queue like hundred people. That's kiasu lor.

The word itself come from Hokkien, "kia" mean fear and "su" mean lose. So literally is fear-lose lor, but now it just mean this competitive, always-must-win kind of vibe. You can kiasu about anything lah — grades, salary, status, everything.

It's not really bad bad, you know. Sometimes kiasu spirit help people work harder and do better. But sometimes also make people stress stress, never relax. So we Singaporeans, we laugh at ourselves about it also lah!
