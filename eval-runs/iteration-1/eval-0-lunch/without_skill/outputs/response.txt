Tanjong Pagar has lots of great options for lunch! Here are some suggestions depending on what you're in the mood for:

**Local Favorites:**
- **Hawker centers** like Amoy Street Food Centre and Club Street have excellent value with traditional dishes like chicken rice, laksa, and char kway teow
- **Nasi Padang** stalls offer variety where you can pick different curries and sides

**Modern Dining:**
- The area has trendy cafes and restaurants along Club Street and Amoy Street
- Many offering Asian fusion, Italian, or contemporary cuisine

**Quick Bites:**
- Plenty of sandwich shops and cafes for a faster lunch
- Food courts in nearby buildings

**My recommendations:**
- If you want authentic and affordable: hit the hawker centers
- If you want something more upscale: explore the restaurants along Club Street
- If you're craving something specific (Japanese, Thai, Chinese, Western), the area has plenty of options
