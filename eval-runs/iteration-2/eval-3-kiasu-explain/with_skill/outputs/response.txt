Wah this one very local sia! Kiasu actually quite hard to explain in English one, but I try lah.

Basically, kiasu means "fear of losing out" — like you always must be first, must get the best, cannot let other people win or have something you don't have. Very competitive one.

You see it everywhere in Singapore meh. Like at hawker centre, people sit down and chope seat with tissue packet — that's kiasu already lor. Cannot let other people take "their" seat. Or like queue for new food drops, free Tian Tian chicken rice at lunch time — confirm kiasu behaviour one. Must get before it run out.

Even parents lah — register their kids for 10 different CCAs "just in case," then the kid completely sian and stressed. That's kiasu parenting lor.

It's not exactly negative one — more like, it's just how Singaporeans are mah. Competitive, want the best, don't like lose. Part of the culture already, haha. But can be funny some time also — like people literally jacking up their car just to fit more cheap petrol from JB. Very kiasu move lor.

So next time you see someone panicking to chope seat, or queue 2 hours for something, you can say "Wah so kiasu one lah" lor. They confirm know what you mean.
